# PRODUCT.md

Product truth lives in `PRD.md`. Short form for design work:

- **What**: national knowledge platform for India's creative economy (research, policy, data, stories, programmes, partnerships).
- **Who**: researchers, policymakers, practitioners (artisans, gurus, creators, studios), industry and technology partners, funders.
- **Mode**: Persuade (home, partners) + Read (research, hub, stories, policy).
- **Platform**: web, mobile first, Next.js 16, bilingual-ready (English now, Hindi next).
- **Non-negotiables**: equal standing of living traditions and contemporary industries; WCAG 2.1 AA; reduced motion; light, dark and auto; no stock photography; placeholders never shipped as fact.
- **Not**: a government portal, a startup landing page, a museum site.
